The valuation

What JK looks at

Price is a judgment call backed by evidence. These are the things that carry the most weight.

  1. 01

    Recent sales of similar homes

    Homes like yours, close to yours, that have actually sold. Asking prices tell you what people hope for; sales tell you what happened.

  2. 02

    Your home's size, layout and lot

    Bedrooms, bathrooms, how the space flows, the lot, parking and anything unusual.

  3. 03

    Condition and updates

    What has been renovated, what is original, and what a buyer will notice in the first five minutes.

  4. 04

    Location and what is nearby

    Street, neighbourhood, schools, transit, highways and the things buyers in that area tend to care about.

  5. 05

    Your competition right now

    The homes buyers will compare yours with, and how yours stacks up against them.

Preparing to list

You do not need to renovate. You do want the home to show well. JK will tell you which of these are worth your time for your home.

  • Declutter and clean. Clear counters, tidy closets and a deep clean make rooms look larger and better cared for.
  • Fix the small things. Dripping taps, loose handles, burnt-out bulbs and scuffed paint tell buyers to look for bigger problems.
  • Neutral and bright. Open the curtains, turn on the lights, and consider fresh paint where walls are bold or worn.
  • Curb appeal. The front door, the lawn and the walkway are the first thing buyers see, and in photos.
  • Gather your paperwork. Property tax bill, utility costs, receipts for work done, and anything you know needs disclosing.
  • Talk about the move. Where are you going next, and when? JK can plan the sale around it.
Listing day onward

Timing and offers

There is rarely a perfect day to list. What matters more is that the home is ready, the price is supported by evidence, and you have a plan for the first couple of weeks.

Choosing how offers are handled

Some sellers set an offer date and review every offer together. Others respond to offers as they arrive. JK will explain both and recommend one for your home and your situation.

Comparing offers properly

The highest number is not always the best offer. Look at the whole thing side by side:

  • The price, and the deposit that comes with it.
  • Conditions, such as financing and inspection, and how long they last.
  • The closing date and whether it fits your move.
  • Anything the buyer is asking you to leave behind or fix.

JK will lay these out in plain terms so you can make the call with a clear head.

Seller FAQ

Questions sellers ask

How do you decide on a price?

JK compares your home with similar homes that have recently sold nearby, then adjusts for the differences: size, condition, updates, lot and location. He also looks at what is currently for sale, because that is your competition. He will show you the reasoning, not just a number.

Should I renovate before I sell?

Usually not big projects. Cleaning, decluttering, small repairs and fresh paint tend to be the sensible first steps. Whether a larger update pays for itself depends on your home, and JK will give you an honest opinion before you spend anything.

What does it cost to sell?

Expect the agent's fee set out in your listing agreement, legal fees, and any costs of paying out your mortgage, which can include a penalty. JK will go through every line with you before you sign so there are no surprises.

Do I have to leave during showings?

Most buyers like to see a home without the owners in it, so it helps if you can step out. JK will work around your schedule, pets and family wherever he can.

Can I sell if I have not found my next home?

Yes, and it is common. The challenge is timing. JK can help you plan the closing date and any conditions so you are not stuck between homes. Tell him your situation in the form above.
