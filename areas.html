Areas

Mississauga andthe communities around it.

These are the areas JK is set to cover. Not sure which one fits you? Tell him how you live, and he will help you narrow it down.

Areas JK serves

  • Mississauga

    A large city on the shore of Lake Ontario, west of Toronto, with a mix of condos, townhomes and detached homes. JK's home base.

  • Brampton

    The neighbouring city to the north of Mississauga, with a wide range of housing from older streets to newer subdivisions.

  • Streetsville

    A village-style community within Mississauga, built around a walkable main street and the Credit River.

  • Erin Mills

    A large residential community in west Mississauga, with established neighbourhoods, parks and shopping close by.

  • Meadowvale

    A planned residential community in northwest Mississauga, known for its green space, trails and quiet streets.

  • Port Credit

    A lakeside neighbourhood where the Credit River meets Lake Ontario, with a waterfront and a village-style main street.

  • Malton

    A community in northeast Mississauga near Toronto Pearson International Airport, with a mix of older and newer homes.

  • Caledon

    North of Brampton, a mix of small towns, rural properties and newer subdivisions for buyers who want more space.
