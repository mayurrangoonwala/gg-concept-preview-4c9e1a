Buyer and seller tips

Good advice,in plain English.

Buying a home is arguably the biggest investment of your life, and selling one is a big decision too. These are the things JK would want you to know before you start.

On this page

  • 8Eight buyer tipsFrom credit score to closing day.
  • 6Six seller tipsPricing, preparing and offers.
For buyers

Buyer tips

  1. 01

    Credit score

    Your credit score is an important factor when a lender decides whether to pre-approve you.

    Read more

    Put simply, the higher your score, the better your chance of being approved. A low score makes a lender less willing to lend, so check yours early and give yourself time to improve it if you need to.

  2. 02

    Mortgage pre-approval

    A pre-approval tells you what you can afford.

    Read more

    Give a lender the documents they ask for and they can point you in the right direction.

    It matters in a competitive market too. Sellers often favour offers with fewer conditions, and being pre-approved can make it easier to waive a financing condition. Waiving also removes your safety net, so talk it through with JK first.

  3. 03

    What kind of home are you looking for?

    Get familiar with homes in your price range, and think about what you need as well as what you want.

    Read more

    Children, highway access, shopping and hospitals are only a few of the things worth weighing up. The right home is the one that fits your life.

  4. 04

    Finding the right agent

    There are plenty of realtors to choose from, and you need the right one for you.

    Read more

    It does not hurt to speak with more than one and see who you connect with. Take time to learn what a realtor owes their client, and do a little research beforehand.

  5. 05

    Communication and research

    Talking clearly with your agent is one of the most important parts of the search.

    Read more

    Say what you are looking for, what you need and what you only want. Your agent should send you potential listings through MLS.

    Do not be shy about looking on your own, and tell your agent what you find. A good agent and client work as a team.

  6. 06

    Found a home you like? Time to make an offer

    This is the most exciting and nerve-racking part.

    Read more

    Sit down with your agent and make a plan. How much do you want to put down? Which conditions do you want, such as financing approval or an inspection?

    Remember that another buyer can offer with no conditions, and sellers often see that as stronger. It is a trade-off, so make it with your eyes open.

  7. 07

    Offer accepted

    Congratulations. As the closing date gets closer, stay on top of things.

    Read more

    You are allowed to visit the home before closing and check whether any problems have come up since your offer was confirmed. If there are issues, contact your agent and your lawyer right away.

  8. 08

    Closing the deal

    Closing is when ownership, the title to the property, is transferred to your name.

    Read more

    Your lawyer takes care of it. If anything is confusing, ask your lawyer or your agent. No question is too small.

For sellers

Seller tips

Most of what makes a sale go well happens before the sign goes up.

  1. 01

    Price it for the market

    The price a buyer will pay is set by what similar homes have sold for, not by what the home cost you or what you need to walk away with. Ask your agent to show the sales behind the number.

  2. 02

    Make it easy to like

    Declutter, clean, fix the small things and let in light. Buyers decide quickly, and most of that decision is how a home feels in the first few minutes.

  3. 03

    Make it easy to see

    Good photos and flexible showing times put your home in front of more buyers. Stepping out during showings helps people picture themselves in it.

  4. 04

    Look at the whole offer

    Compare price, deposit, conditions and the closing date side by side. The highest price is not always the best offer once everything is on the table.

  5. 05

    Know your costs

    Ask about the agent's fee, legal fees and any mortgage payout costs up front, so there are no surprises on closing day.

  6. 06

    Plan the move, not just the sale

    Know where you are going and when. The closing date, any conditions and your next home all need to fit together. Tell your agent early.
