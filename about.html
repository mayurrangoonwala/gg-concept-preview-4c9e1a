The agent

Who you'll be working with

JK Gill is part of the Gill and Gill Team at RE/MAX Gold Realty in Mississauga. As a realtor, he sets out to use his wide-ranging industry knowledge and experience to get clients what they want when they buy or sell a home.

Some of his clients are first-time buyers looking for a starter home. Others are established families looking for the dream home. JK works with both, and tailors his advice to fit each.

He is a former athlete, and he brings that drive to the job. Determination matters in this business. To succeed you have to be committed, driven and persistent, and JK holds himself to that.

His formula for success has always been honesty, hard work and commitment, and it is the same approach he takes with clients. He pays close attention to their needs, wants and interests so that, as a team, they can work through whatever stands in the way.

JK believes honesty earns respect, and respect builds trust. In any relationship, personal or professional, trust matters most. That is why he is committed to protecting his clients' interests and dealing with them honestly, fairly and respectfully. His goal is to be the realtor his clients recommend to the people they know.

What to expect

Working with JK

Every client's situation is different. This is the general shape of the process.

  1. 01

    A first conversation

    You tell JK what you are trying to do and why. He asks questions about your timing, your budget or your home, and about what matters most to you.

  2. 02

    A plan before the action

    Before showings or a listing, you agree on a plan: price range, areas, timeline and how you would like to be kept up to date.

  3. 03

    Straight advice at each decision

    When it is time to choose a price, make an offer or answer one, JK lays out the options, what he would do and why. The decision stays yours.

  4. 04

    Coordination to closing

    JK keeps the dates and paperwork moving with your lawyer and lender, and is there for questions up to closing day.
